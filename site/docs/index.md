# Getting started

Sign in to Common Ink, write your first note, learn the parts of the app, and connect an AI agent to your notes.

## What Common Ink is

Common Ink is a markdown notes app at commonink.app that you share with your AI agents, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other MCP client. You and your agents read and edit the same notes, and every change records who made it.

## Sign in

Common Ink is in early access, so only accounts on the early access list can sign in. To join the list, write to support@commonink.app.

1. Go to https://commonink.app.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**.
3. Pick the Google account that is on the list.

If you pick an account that isn't on the list, Common Ink says so on a page titled "Not on the list". Choose **Use another account** to try a different one.

## Write your first note

The first time you sign in, Common Ink makes a workspace for you, named for your first name, such as "Ada's workspace". A workspace holds your notes, folders, and uploads. You are its admin. It starts with one folder, Unsorted.

The app opens on your Dashboard. To write a note:

1. Choose **New note** in the sidebar.
2. Type. The note starts empty, with the cursor in it.
3. Give it a title. A note's title is its first `#` heading. Until it has one, its first line is its title.

Common Ink saves as you type. A note that you never typed anything into is discarded when you close its tab. A note made with no folder open goes in Unsorted. To learn what a note can hold, see [Writing notes](/docs/notes).

## Find your way around

The sidebar is on the left. The workspace menu at its top switches between your workspaces and opens **Agents**, **Members**, **Sharing**, and **Settings**. Under it are links to pages such as Dashboard, Search, Todos, Today, and Calendar. **More** shows the rest, such as History, Uploads, Trash, and Plugins. Below the links is the folder tree, which starts with All notes.

Every note, folder, and page opens in a tab. A single click opens a preview tab, shown in italics, and the next single click replaces it. To keep a tab open, double-click it or edit the note. To see two tabs side by side, drag a tab to an edge of the editor, or, when the pane has more than one tab, choose **Split right** or **Split down** from the tab's menu.

The inspector is on the right. Its bar opens the History, Backlinks, and Info panels for whatever is in front.

These keys run the commands you use most. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl in place of Cmd.

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Cmd+Shift+P` | Opens the command palette, which lists every command |
| `Cmd+P` | Opens quick open, which finds a note or upload by its title or name |
| `Cmd+\` | Shows or hides the sidebar |
| `Cmd+.` | Turns focus on or off, so one tab fills the window |
| `?` | Shows every keyboard shortcut, when you aren't typing |

You can change any of these in Settings, under Keyboard shortcuts.

## Connect your agent

An agent reads and edits your notes as you, with your role in each workspace and no more. Its changes are recorded under its own name, so you can see what it did and revert it.

To connect one, open the workspace menu, choose **Agents**, name the agent, and paste the prompt Common Ink gives you into it. Claude and ChatGPT in the browser connect differently. You add Common Ink as a connector in the app, then allow it. [Connect an agent](/docs/agents) covers every client.
