# Privacy policy

Common Ink keeps what it needs to run your notebook, and shows your notes only to the people and agents you let in. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to have it removed.

Effective October 19, 2026.

## The short version

- We keep your name, email address and picture from Google, and what you put in Common Ink: notes, files, folders and their history.
- If you connect Google Calendar or Contacts, we read them when you or your agent ask, and we don't keep a copy.
- People you share with see what you share. Agents you connect can do what you let them do, and never more than you can.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, use tracking cookies, or train AI models on your notes.
- To get a copy of your data, or to have it deleted, write to [support@commonink.app](mailto:support@commonink.app).

## Who we are

Common Ink, at commonink.app, is run by Audrow Nash LLC ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to [support@commonink.app](mailto:support@commonink.app).

## What we collect

**Your Google account.** When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address, profile picture and account ID.

**Google Calendar and Contacts, if you allow them.** Common Ink can ask Google for access to your calendars and contacts, and Google lets you allow or refuse each one. When you allow them, we keep the access Google gives us, encrypted, and use it to read your events and contacts when you or an agent you connected ask for them. With calendar access, you and your agents can also create and change events. With full contacts access, you can create and change contacts, and your agents can read them. We read events and contacts from Google each time and don't store them, except for what you choose to write into a note, such as a person's name in a mention.

**What you put in Common Ink.** Notes, uploaded files, folders, workspaces, the people you invite, what you share and with whom, your settings, and the plugins you install.

**History.** Each change to a note records who made it (you, someone you work with, an agent or a plugin), when, and what the note said before, so changes can be seen and reverted.

**Agents and apps you connect.** Each agent's name, the access you gave it, when it was last used, and a hash of its token. We never keep the token itself. For an app that connects with a sign-in, such as Claude or ChatGPT in the browser, we also keep the name and address it registered with.

**The web clipper.** When you clip a page with the Common Ink Clipper for Chrome, it sends us what you chose to clip: the page's text, the address it came from, and any images in it, which become a note and its uploads in the folder you pick. The clipper sends nothing until you clip, and it sends only to the Common Ink address it is signed in to.

**Technical records.** Cloudflare, which hosts Common Ink, handles every request, and may keep records such as IP addresses for security under its own policy. Before Common Ink keeps a log line, it removes tokens, email addresses, share links and anything an error quotes. Our logs don't record the address of each request.

## How we use it

- To sign you in and show you the workspaces you belong to.
- To store, search, display and sync your notes and files, for you and for the people and agents you share them with.
- To read and change your calendar and contacts when you or your agent ask.
- To keep Common Ink secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
- To contact you about your account or about changes to Common Ink.

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use your notes, files, calendar or contacts to train AI models.

## Google user data

Common Ink's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

We use your Google data only to provide the features you can see in Common Ink: signing you in, showing your name and picture to the people you work with, and showing and changing your calendar and contacts when you or your agent ask. We don't transfer it to anyone except to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of Common Ink, and we never use it for advertising. No person at Common Ink reads it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

You can disconnect Calendar or Contacts in Common Ink's settings, and remove Common Ink's access at any time in your [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Who can see your content

- **You.** A private note is seen by you alone, even in a shared workspace.
- **The people you work with.** Members of a workspace see the notes in it according to their role. A guest sees only the notes and folders shared with them. Your calendar and contacts are never shown to anyone else in a workspace.
- **Anyone with a link you made.** A share link lets anyone, or anyone signed in, read the note or folder it is for, until you turn it off.
- **Agents you connect.** An agent reads and writes with the access you gave it. What it reads goes to the company that runs it, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, under that company's terms and privacy policy.
- **Plugins.** A plugin runs only after an admin of the workspace approves its code and you turn it on. Most plugins run in a sandbox and can read or change only what their permissions allow. A plugin allowed to reach the network can send what it reads to the sites it names. A plugin given full trust runs in your session and can do anything you can.
- **Our service providers**, listed below, which handle data only to run Common Ink for us.
- **The law.** We may disclose information when we believe the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users, the public or us.

## Service providers

- **Cloudflare** hosts Common Ink and stores its database and files on its network, which may be outside your country.
- **Google** provides sign-in, your profile picture, and your calendar and contacts if you connect them.

## Embeds and other sites

A YouTube video, a post on X or Bluesky, or another embed in a note loads from that service in your browser, which may collect data or set cookies under its own policy. A YouTube video loads only after you press play. The built-in Weather plugin, when you add it to your dashboard, sends your approximate location to Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud to look up the weather.

## Cookies and local storage

Common Ink uses two cookies, both needed for it to work:

- `__Host-ink_session` keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days.
- `__Host-ink_google` protects the step where Google signs you in, and expires after 10 minutes.

Your browser's local storage keeps preferences such as your open tabs and the size of panels. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

- Your account, notes and files are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
- When you put a note or file in Trash, we remove it for good after 30 days, with its history, or at once if you delete it forever.
- A note's earlier versions stay in its history while the note exists, so changes can be reverted.
- Our database backups keep a copy of deleted data for up to 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your personal information by writing to [support@commonink.app](mailto:support@commonink.app). We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as to object to or restrict how we use your information, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

You can disconnect any agent in Common Ink at any time, and sign out everywhere from Settings.

## Security

Common Ink encrypts all traffic to and from it. We encrypt Google access tokens before storing them, and store only a hash of each agent token. Common Ink checks every request against what its person or agent may reach. An uploaded file opens in a sandbox, so it can't run code in your session. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you promptly. To report a security problem, see our [security policy](https://github.com/paudrow/common-ink-v3/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Children

Common Ink is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you in Common Ink or by email before it takes effect, and change the date at the top of this page.
